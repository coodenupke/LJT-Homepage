I am a first-year PhD candidate at HKUST NLP Group, supervised by Prof. Junxian He. I graduated from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU) in June 2024. My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning.

## Academic Background

- **Ph.D. in Computer Science** (2024–Present) — Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), supervised by Prof. Junxian He.
- **B.Eng.** (2020–2024) — Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU).
